Do you take walk-ins?
Always. Walk-ins are welcome and regulars are seen first. If you want a guaranteed time, book online or call ahead — it only takes a few seconds.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the live calendar above (powered by Square, open 24/7) or call/text (701) 610-0780. Pick your service, choose a time, and you're locked in.
What's the difference between a fade and a regular cut?
A fade blends the hair down to the skin for that seamless taper. Any blade above a #1 — where the sides keep some length — is a regular cut. Not sure? Branden will dial it in when you sit down.
Do you cut kids' hair?
Absolutely. Kids 12 & under have their own pricing, and we do custom designs too. First haircut? We'll take care of them.
What payment do you accept?
Card, tap-to-pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Square, plus cash. Tips are always appreciated and can be added at checkout.
Can I get a cut after hours?
Yes — after-hours cuts are available by request at a premium rate. Reach out through the booking page or give the shop a call to set it up.
Where are you located?
